Methodology

  • Reading the chart: bars diverge from the center line — left is input price, right is output price, both on logarithmic scales. The cached-input price is embedded inside the input bar as the solid segment.
  • Prices are official list prices collected from each provider's pricing page — short-context tier where tiered, off-peak where peak/off-peak pricing exists, promotional discounts ignored.
  • CNY-priced models are converted to USD at the Bank of China mid-rate on the collection date (rate recorded per model).
  • «—» means the provider publishes no cached-input price for that model.
  • This page has no auto-update mechanism; refreshes follow a manual collection protocol.
